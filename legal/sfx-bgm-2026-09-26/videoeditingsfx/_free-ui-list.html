Frequently asked questions

What are UI sound effects used for?
UI sounds — clicks, chimes, notifications and pops — make app demos, screen recordings and motion-graphics interfaces feel responsive, and double as crisp accents for text overlays.
Are these UI sound effects really free?
Yes. All 19 ui sounds are released under a CC0 (public-domain) license — free to download as individual MP3s with no sign-up, and free for personal and commercial projects.
Will these ui sounds get my video copyright-claimed?
No. None of the ui sounds are registered with Content ID, so they won't trigger copyright claims or strikes on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram Reels or anywhere else — monetized videos included, no attribution required.
What format are the ui sounds, and which editors work?
Each ui sound downloads as a standard MP3 (the full pack also includes 48kHz/24-bit WAV). They drag into CapCut, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro, After Effects and any editor.
